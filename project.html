About the Project

Global Fuel Analytics is a data visualization project developed using Tableau. It focuses on analyzing fuel consumption, petrol and diesel prices, CO2 emissions, and renewable energy share across different countries and regions.

Interactive charts and filters help users explore the available data and understand patterns over time.
